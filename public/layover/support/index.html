Support

Offline games for flights, trains and anywhere without signal. Need help or found a bug? Email baileyconradt@yahoo.com and include your device model and what happened.

Common questions

Does Layover need an internet connection?

No. Maps, flags, phrases and fonts are all built into the app. Everything works in Airplane Mode.

Photo Sweep deleted something I wanted

Deleted photos go to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app for 30 days. Open Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted, select the photo and tap Recover.

Photo Sweep didn't free up any space

iPhone only frees the space once Recently Deleted is emptied (or after 30 days). To free it now, open Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted → Select → Delete All.

Photos say "In iCloud" and won't copy or preview

With "Optimize iPhone Storage" on, some originals live only in iCloud. Copying, sharing and video previews need to download them, so connect to the internet and try again. Swiping to keep or delete works offline either way.

The speaker button is missing in Phrasebook

Pronunciation uses your phone's built-in voices. Add a voice for that language in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices (iPhone) or your Android text-to-speech settings, then reopen the language.

Where are my scores stored?

Only on your device. There are no accounts. Deleting the app deletes your scores.
