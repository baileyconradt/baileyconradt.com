Layover: Offline Games

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Layover collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Everything stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

  • Game results (scores, which mode and region you played) are saved only on your device so you can see your stats. They are never uploaded.
  • Photo Sweep remembers which photos you chose to keep, so they don't come back in later sweeps. This list of photo identifiers is saved only on your device.

Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

Your photos (Photo Sweep, iPhone)

If you use Photo Sweep, Layover asks for access to your photo library so it can show your photos and videos and delete the ones you choose. Your photos are never uploaded, copied to a server or shared with anyone. Photos are only deleted after you confirm in the system's own dialog, and deleted items go to your Recently Deleted album, where you can restore them for 30 days. If you add photos to the "Sweep Favorites" album, that album lives in your Photos library like any other.

You can change or remove photo access at any time in your device's Settings.

Pronunciation audio (Phrasebook)

Phrasebook reads phrases aloud with your device's built-in text-to-speech voices. The speech is generated on your device by the operating system; Layover does not send the text anywhere.

Children

Layover does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

Layover contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software. Apple and Google may collect information about app downloads and crashes under their own privacy policies, independently of Layover.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
